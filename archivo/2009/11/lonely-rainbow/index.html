Lonely rainbow

3 de noviembre de 2009

September is the rainy season, but, what the heck? November can be rainy too… I guess that’s what the clouds where thinking when they decided to start raining this morning.

I just love the sound of rain in the morning.

Specially when the wind blows the raindrops out of the window so I can leave it open and just sit and watch how the backyard gets wet; so lovely.

Some raindrops on the balcony, some raindrops on the trees, a raindrop occasionally gets on my knee.

I like when it rains in the morning, November can be rainy too, I like how it get fresh after the rain.

Is not a good day to wash your clothes today.
