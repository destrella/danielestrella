Where Is the Today?

22 de octubre de 2009

I woke up… It was about 1pm… everything was quiet… I ate and checked the email… something was different… I couldn’t stay awake, my body felt to weak so I went back to the hammock.

A message on the mirror; the room it’s so small, did I wake up again? It’s about 6.33pm now. What is missing? The room is so small. The kitchen is empty. The roof is so quiet. Everything is so quiet. Emptiness. Something is missing.

Russian songs, empty hammock, old bikini, message on the mirror, vestiges of yesterday, traces… where’s the today?

I need some tea; is it my back straight? I ate meat. Where is the today?

Did I wake up? Broken links…
